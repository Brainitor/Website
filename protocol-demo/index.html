Scientific review guide

What this build demonstrates

A shortened, interactive review of three visual memory paradigms mapped from Polk et al. (2026).

Purpose

This demo lets us verify the participant flow, response mechanics, event capture, and scoring logic before building a study-ready implementation. It does not provide a diagnosis or a validated cognitive result.

What is implemented

MDT-OS Snapshot
Exact repeat versus subtle lure judgments, change localization, and shortened one-back and interleaved two-back phases.
ORR Room Recall
Two object-location associations per room, followed by immediate and delayed three-choice recall.
CSR Scene Memory
Inside or outside encoding, a delayed recognition phase, and yes, no, or unsure responses for old and new scenes.

How to review it

  1. Choose one of the three protocols in the left navigation.
  2. Leave Accelerated walkthrough off to review mapped stimulus exposure. Turn it on only to move through MDT-OS and ORR more quickly.
  3. Complete the participant flow inside the phone. Long delay gates use a clearly labeled manual simulation in this demo.
  4. Use the scientist inspector on the right, or below on smaller screens, to check protocol provenance, event telemetry, and scoring.

Review boundaries

Trial counts are abbreviated. The AI-generated scenes, composite rooms, MDT hit regions, and 500 ms neutral interval between CSR scenes are demo assets or implementation assumptions. Full stimulus matching, calibration, quality-control rules, and clinical validation are still required before research or clinical use.

No per-trial correctness or participant-facing score is shown.
